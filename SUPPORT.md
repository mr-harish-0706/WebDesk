## 💬 Community Channels

- **GitHub Discussions**: For general questions, feature ideas, show & tell, and installation troubleshooting.
- **GitHub Issues**: For verified bug reports and technical issues.

---

## 🐛 Submitting a Bug Report

When opening a bug report on GitHub:
- Specify your Linux distribution and desktop environment (e.g., Ubuntu 24.04 GNOME, Fedora 40 KDE).
- Provide Electron console logs or terminal output.
- List exact steps to reproduce.
